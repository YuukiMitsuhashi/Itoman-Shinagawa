AI pipeline

1 · Source — YouTube metadata + local clip extraction
2 · Detection — high-resolution tiled swimmer detection
3 · Tracking — appearance + motion + pose continuity
4 · Pose — enlarged swimmer crops for small subjects
5 · Geometry — camera motion + perspective + angled wall keyframes
6 · Swimming model — stroke, turns, lengths, speed, stroke cycles
7 · Confidence — uncertainty and manual correction points

Wall keyframes

Walls are not assumed to be fixed. Add a wall observation at different video times, then set its angle. The AI interpolates camera/wall geometry between keyframes.
