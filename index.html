Workflow

  1. 1. Load a video (left)
  2. 2. Click the swimmer to track
  3. 3. Calibrate real-world distance
  4. 4. Mark wall / lane length
  5. 5. Run analysis

2. Select swimmer

Scrub to a frame where your swimmer is clearly visible (ideally above water), then click "Detect people", then click on that person in the video.

3. Calibrate scale

Click two points along the swim direction whose real-world distance you know (e.g. two lane rope markers, or wall-to-wall on the pool floor).

4. Race setup

Telling it the stroke and distance replaces the guesswork — for IM it assumes the standard equal-length order Fly → Back → Breast → Free based on total distance ÷ pool length ÷ 4.

5. Run analysis

This steps through the video frame-by-frame running pose detection — a few minutes of footage can take a couple of minutes to process. Keep this tab in front (some browsers throttle background tabs).

Notes on accuracy

• Speed/splits are only as good as your calibration — use two points that lie along the swimmer's actual line of travel, and try to film from directly to the side when possible.

• Underwater kick counts and underwater distance are the least reliable numbers here — pose models struggle with refraction/blur underwater, so treat those as estimates to sanity-check by eye, not ground truth.

• Stroke classification is a heuristic (arm-symmetry + body orientation), not a trained classifier. It'll usually get freestyle/backstroke vs breaststroke/butterfly right; fly vs breast is the shakiest split.
